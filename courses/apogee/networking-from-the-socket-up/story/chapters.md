# a-socket-of-your-own

## Briefing

Monday 6 March, at the bench in the hut. Ystmark's dish controllers are worn out: Dish 2's lost its position twice in February. Sunniva needs dishd talking to the maker's simulator before Dish 1's old controller comes out on Monday 20 March. Ystmark sees every polar orbit on almost every revolution, and a controller nobody can talk to is a dish nobody can use.

## Debrief

Friday afternoon, dishd said hello and the simulator answered: `YST-1 firmware 2.4, last command 0`. Sunniva wrote to Port Ellery: "Minus 18. Calm. Simulator talking to dishd." Nkem answered in one line: "Good. Next week, over your test line."

# where-one-message-ends

## Briefing

Monday 13 March, in the hut. Sunniva's test line now runs from the simulator to Port Ellery and back over the backhaul, as slow as a storm day: answers arrive in pieces, or two at once. Nkem wants dishd reading every message whole before Dish 1's new controller goes in next Monday. A message lost on the line can hide a dish that has stowed itself.

## Debrief

Friday 17 March: dishd's session over the slow line came through whole, every answer paired with its request, and this time the simulated Dish 1's stow showed on the screen. Nkem: "Whole frames, every time." On Monday, Dish 1's new controller goes into its radome.

# bytes-in-order

## Briefing

Monday 27 March, in Dish 1's radome and the hut. Dish 1's new controller went in last week, and its first status frames, read as the manual's table says, put the dish at 382.15° in a 3,277 km/h wind. Tomás flies up on Tuesday to point it true. On Friday at 07:02, Cirrine-1's pass will be the first customer pass on the new controller.

## Debrief

06:54 on Friday 31 March: Dish 1 reported 183.25° and 41.70°, exactly where Tomás had asked, and Cirrine-1's 07:02 pass came down whole on the new controller. Sunniva: "Wind 12. Dish 1 true. Storm due on the 11th."

# deadlines-on-the-wire

## Briefing

Tuesday 11 April, the hut, after the first spring storm. At 03:10 the fiber dropped, and dishd waited forty minutes on a connection that was already dead, so Dish 1 sat still through Cirrine-1's 03:43 pass. Ingrid wants every wait on the line to end in seconds. Yusra: "TL;DR: Cirrine lost its 03:43 pass and asks what stops it happening again."

## Debrief

Friday evening, Sunniva's replay of Tuesday's storm ended in five seconds, not forty minutes, with one line in Ingrid's log: `YST-1: no answer in 5s`. Yusra sent it on to Cirrine. Sunniva: "Wind 20, falling. Dish 2's controller goes in Monday. Then two dishes to watch."

# both-dishes-at-once

## Briefing

Monday 24 April, the hut, under the midnight sun. Dish 2's new controller went in last week, and Sunniva needs dishd to run both dishes at once and show them on its watch port: on the terminal in the hut, Ingrid's screen in the Fishbowl and Tomás's laptop. One dish's trouble must never stop the other, and Friday's deploy must leave nothing hanging.

## Debrief

Friday at ten to midnight, the sun still on the ridge, both dishes scrolled past on Sunniva's terminal, a line each every second, and the deploy had left nothing hanging. Sunniva: "Sun up, wind 20. Two dishes, one dishd." Nkem: "Good. And when the line drops?"

# when-the-line-drops

## Briefing

Tuesday 9 May, after the storm. The island had no line from 07:22 to 07:36, and dishd gave up and never dialed again; Ingrid restarted it too late for Cirrine-1's 07:28 pass. The restart sent Tomás's calibration nudge to Dish 2 a second time, and Furrow-A's 07:52 pass came down at a sixteenth of its strength. Sunniva: "Link down 14 min. Dish 2 waiting on your code. Coffee low."

## Debrief

Friday, 10:14: the line came back, dishd was through within half a minute, and Dish 2 had jogged exactly once. Sunniva: "Calm. Line out 14 min. dishd back alone. One jog. Coffee restocked." The next message from the hut is about Tuesday's recordings.

# requests-from-scratch

## Briefing

Monday 22 May, in the hut. On 9 May the fiber stayed down until 19:40, and recordings had nowhere to wait but the recorder's small disk, which filled at 13:20: Furrow-B's 13:21 pass was lost. Hollis: "LOST FURROW-B 13:21. FIELD 7. WHY." Sunniva wants a station server in the hut to keep every recording until headquarters collects it, and Nkem says: build it on the socket you know.

## Debrief

Friday 26 May: the station server answered its first requests in the hut, the recorder's with the list of recordings and every crooked one with its refusal and the reason. Mirela signed it off: "LGTM once it fails closed." In June, the recordings themselves.

# bodies-and-chunks

## Briefing

Monday 5 June, in the hut. Yusra's TL;DR from Furrowlight: "Ten Furrow passes a day booked over Ystmark, all June." The recorder will stream each one into the station server as it comes down, its CRC last. A recording kept short or damaged is a field that Furrowlight's farmers can't plan by.

## Debrief

Friday evening, all ten of the day's booked Furrow passes were in the station server, each checked against its CRC, and no cut stream has been kept since Tuesday. Hollis answered in capitals: "TEN FOR TEN." Ingrid's question for next week: how soon does headquarters hear of each one?

# without-a-connection

## Briefing

Monday 12 June, in the hut, the sun never setting. Ingrid's question starts before LOS: the Fishbowl hears nothing of a Furrow pass until its recording lands, and on 6 June it learned of the recorder's restart from a cut upload. She wants every frame of every pass on her screen as it comes down, and to ask the recorder, whenever the frames stop, what it is doing.

## Debrief

Friday, 08:00:56, five seconds after LOS: Furrow-A's pass had crossed Ingrid's screen frame by frame, in order, none twice, three lost on the line, and the recorder answered `IDLE` at the first try. Ingrid: "Copy. Next week: the recordings, the moment they land."

# answers-from-scratch

## Briefing

Monday 19 June, in the hut. From 06:00 tomorrow the station server takes every pass, and Ingrid wants headquarters' collector to hear of each recording at once and fetch it over one open line. Furrow-A's 06:32 pass is the first Furrowlight pass through it, and the booking service has promised Furrowlight its image within a minute of LOS.

## Debrief

At 06:40:36 the feed told Port Ellery of Furrow-A's pass; at 06:40:39 the booking service told Furrowlight its image had landed. Sunniva: "Midnight sun. Both dishes. Every byte. Coffee for two." Mirela's note: anyone tapping the island's line reads every byte, and Cirrine wants its commands readable by Cirrine alone.
