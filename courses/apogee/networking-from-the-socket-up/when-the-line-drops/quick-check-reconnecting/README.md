Wednesday evening. Before dishd is trusted with a storm on its own, Ingrid sends three questions from the Fishbowl, Apogee's glass-walled ops room at Port Ellery, about its waits between dials:

```text
failed dials in a row   wait
1                       0.5 to 1 s
2                       1 to 2 s
5                       8 to 16 s
6 and every one after   15 to 30 s
```

## Your task

Answer Ingrid's three questions: why each wait carries a random factor, why the waits stop growing at 30 seconds, and what a context canceled during a wait means.
