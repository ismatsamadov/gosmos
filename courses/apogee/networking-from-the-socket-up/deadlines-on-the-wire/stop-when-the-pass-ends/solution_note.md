The deadline set to now wakes the waiting read the moment the pass's context ends, and `ctx.Err()` then says why it stopped: `context.DeadlineExceeded` at LOS. Calling `stop` unhooks the context once the read is done, and waiting when it reports `false` lets a deadline already on its way land before any later read sets its own. The check at the top answers an ended pass without reading at all.
