The test van is home, and the night shift wants gctl without starting it again for every command. Over one week at Port Ellery you build one program, a lesson at a time: a prompt that reads commands and splits them into words, a table of commands that explains itself, the night's passes and stations, a stow that `next` respects, a cache that knows when its passes are stale and stays safe beside a background refresher, settings from a file and a history that survives a restart. On Friday night the whole session is checked against a transcript, and Ingrid runs a pass from your prompt. Standard library only.
