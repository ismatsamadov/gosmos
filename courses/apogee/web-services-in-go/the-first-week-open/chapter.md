The first customers' week: file uploads read from `multipart` forms within a limit and sniffed with `http.DetectContentType`, ranged downloads that resume through `http.ServeContent`, download links signed with HMAC that expire, webhooks refused inside the network by a `net.Dialer`'s `Control` hook, redirects that stay on the site, and a monthly plan on an injected clock.
