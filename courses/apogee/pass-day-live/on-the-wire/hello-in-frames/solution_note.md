HELLO goes out in one write with its CRC over the type, seq and body, and the frames after it come through the reader that read the banner, so a WELCOME sent along with the banner is never lost. A goroutine of its own reads them and hands them over a channel, so the pass waits for a frame or its LOS in one `select`, as later lessons will need, and a WELCOME, a NAK's code or a BYE `stowed` decides how the pass ends.
