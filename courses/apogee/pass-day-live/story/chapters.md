# the-weeks-plan

## Briefing

Monday 2 July, at the backend desks. In three weeks the investors watch every pass of the network for a week, and Tomás and Ingrid want that week flown first. The rehearsal must say what it flies, plan the week for twenty stations from passplan's archive, and check Yusra's bookings: a booked pass with no dish is lost before the week begins.

## Debrief

By Tuesday noon the plan holds 14,297 contacts and every booking has its dish. Yusra struck two from her export: Hollis's Furrow-A over Cape Wren at 04:58:30, copied from an old email, and one for Thistle, which re-entered in 2027. Ingrid: "Copy. Tomorrow it flies."

# a-clock-for-every-pass

## Briefing

Wednesday 4 July, in the Fishbowl. The week is planned, and Ingrid wants to watch it fly: every pass on the screen as it starts and ends, the whole week in seconds so she can run it again after every change, and a way to stop it. A pass that outlives its LOS holds its dish, as stuck passes jammed Lantern Atoll in February.

## Debrief

By Thursday evening the whole week flies in seconds, every pass from its AOS to its LOS. Calidra Atoll's silent calls end at each LOS, and Ingrid's stop leaves nothing behind. Sunniva: "Wind 12. Every call on time. Radios still waiting for a hello."

# on-the-wire

## Briefing

Friday 6 July, at the backend desks, with Sunniva on the link from Ystmark. The calls reach every radio; now the rehearsal must speak the radios' frames and keep every satellite's newest telemetry for the Board, where Sunniva and Joss will read it. Thistle-2's battery sensor still glitches, Varsk Island's line flips bits, and in a storm Ystmark's fiber dies without a sound.

## Debrief

By Monday evening every pass says hello, every damaged frame is dropped and a dead line is called back until it answers. Sunniva: "Wind 9. Fiber cut at 03:31, as scripted. One Pipit-2 pass lost. The rest flew the backhaul."

# signed-and-sealed

## Briefing

Tuesday 10 July, at Mirela's desk. The investors' security reviewer will read every command sent up, and asked to see a key replaced in the live week: `k-2029-07` takes over from `k-2028-10` at 00:00 on Wednesday 25 July. Every command must be signed with its satellite's own key, Cirrine's sealed, and none run twice. A satellite that misses its new keys can't be commanded once `k-2028-10` retires on 1 August.

## Debrief

By Wednesday evening every command goes up signed or sealed, one at a time per satellite, and none runs twice. In the rehearsal all eighty satellites hold `k-2029-07` by Monday 09:27:31. Mirela: "LGTM once it fails closed. It does." Tomorrow, Yusra's files.

# every-file-delivered

Thursday 12 July, at Yusra's desk. The investors will ask customers how fast their data arrives, so every file must come down whole and reach its customer within a minute. In the week as Ingrid writes it, Furrow-K's image over Vorrel Island loses three chunks, and Cirrine's receiver restarts at 09:32 on Tuesday for a new version. Joss has Kessick's first receiver ready for Thistle-2's first science.

## Debrief

By Friday evening every file of the week lands whole and reaches its customer: Furrow-K's image at 08:12:30 on Tuesday, Thistle-2's first science at Kessick at 16:04:25 on Thursday. Yusra: "TL;DR: 1,484 files, 1,484 events, none lost." On Monday, Sunniva's forecast.

# the-storm

## Briefing

Monday 16 July, in the Fishbowl. A storm is coming to Ystmark on Thursday 26 July, the middle of the investors' week. Ingrid wants the rehearsal to log, count and page through it as the real week would, and to end in the week's report, which she reads at the full rehearsal on Friday at 15:00: "Every pass counted, or we don't show them."

## Debrief

On Thursday 26 July the storm comes as Sunniva said: Ystmark stows at 10:50, and the pager rings once, for you. By 00:03 on Monday 30 July every booked pass of the investors' week has flown. Ingrid writes one line in the log: "Every pass counts. Every pass counted."
