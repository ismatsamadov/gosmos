short: Rehearse Apogee's whole ground segment for a week in one Go program, against twenty simulated stations and eighty satellites.
---
Apogee's investors will watch the whole network for a week and want every booked pass flown, so Ingrid wants that week flown first. You build the rehearsal: one program that plans the week for twenty stations from passplan's archive and the customers' bookings, runs every pass on its own clock, takes telemetry in over the wire, signs and seals every command and replaces a key mid-week, reassembles every file and delivers it by signed webhook, and logs, counts and pages through a storm at Ystmark, ending in the week's report. Everything runs in simulated time: a week in seconds. Standard library only.
