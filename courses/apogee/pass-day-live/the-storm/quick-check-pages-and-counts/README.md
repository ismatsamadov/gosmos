Thursday 19 July. Tomás asks what the investors will see on Friday, and wants the reason behind every number.

A stowed dish refuses its passes: they are skipped, not failed. The week's report counts every booked pass not flown as lost, and a station's error budget is its booked passes times what its target allows, in whole passes. Ystmark has 210 bookings in the week and a 99.5% target.

## Your task

Answer Tomás's questions: what the passes skipped in the wind lock do to the pager and to the report, how many passes Ystmark may lose, and where the report's numbers come from.
