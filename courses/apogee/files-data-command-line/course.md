The test van climbs the Quebrada Añil to recalibrate Salar Quieto's dishes, with days at a time and no signal. With Paz in the van and Nkem reviewing the code, you build gctl from files alone: whole files, folders and safe saves; readers and writers; logs read line by line; bookings in JSON and spreadsheets in CSV, odd ones included; times that mean the same instant everywhere; one command with flags, subcommands and exit statuses; a station table that travels inside the program; and parcels in XML, zip and tar, unpacked safely. At the top of the road, a whole calibration day runs from gctl and files alone. Standard library only, with every test on isolated files and streams.
