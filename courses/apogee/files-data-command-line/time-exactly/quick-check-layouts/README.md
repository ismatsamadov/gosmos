The van stops at a bend with two bars of signal, long enough for Nkem to read the timestamp code. Her review is three questions, asked one at a time, before anything else depends on these helpers.

A layout is the reference time, Monday 2 January 2006 at 15:04:05, written the way you want your time to look. An offset such as `-07:00` says how a time is written, not which instant it is.

## Your task

Answer three questions: one about writing a layout, one about two readings of the same pass, and one about what `UTC` changes.
