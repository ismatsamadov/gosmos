Monday afternoon in the Fishbowl. Tomás taps the whiteboard, where the morning's targets are still written: 9950 at every station, 9900 at Lantern Atoll. "A promise is only as good as the room it leaves. Nobody builds a bridge for exactly the heaviest truck!" Before Yusra sends the targets to customers, he has three questions for you.

## Your task

Answer Tomás's three questions: what a target of 100% would leave Lantern Atoll, how many of Port Ellery's 1,104 passes in a quarter 99.5% lets it lose, and what a budget still unspent at the end of a quarter is for.
