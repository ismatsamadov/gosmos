You join Apogee's on-call rota, and on your second night the pager wakes you at 02:46: one pass in five at Lantern Atoll is failing, the caretaker sees nothing wrong, and the log says only "error". With Nkem reviewing, you make the programs explain themselves: structured, leveled logs that carry their pass through every program and keep every secret out; log files that never fill a disk; counters, gauges and histograms you can trust; alert rules that wake the right person once; error budgets counted in whole passes; CPU and heap profiles, benchmarks and allocation counts; and traces that follow one request from headquarters to an island. By February the night shift calls you, and you find what has been failing Lantern Atoll's passes since October. Standard library only, every check on your own Mac.
