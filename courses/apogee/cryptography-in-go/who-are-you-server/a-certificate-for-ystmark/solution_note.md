Signed by the stations CA's key, with that CA's certificate as its parent, Ystmark's certificate chains through `Apogee Stations CA 2028` to the root. Its one extended key usage, server authentication, means `Verify` refuses it wherever a client must be proven, and without `IsCA` it can sign nothing that anyone will trust. Checking the issuer first turns a CA without its key into an error instead of a panic.
