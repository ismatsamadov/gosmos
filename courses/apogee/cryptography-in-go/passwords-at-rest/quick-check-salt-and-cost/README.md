Tuesday. Ingrid writes up Sunday's laptop for the log, one line per change, and she writes nothing she can't explain. "Copy: salt and 600,000 iterations. Before either goes in the log, tell me what each one buys us against whoever has the laptop."

She has the user file's new rules on her clipboard: a fresh 16-byte salt for every password, stored with its record; every key derived at 600,000 iterations.

## Your task

Answer Ingrid's three questions: what a salt stops on its own, what 600,000 iterations cost someone holding the user file, and whether the salt must be kept secret.
