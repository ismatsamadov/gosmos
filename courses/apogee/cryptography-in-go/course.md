Cirrine Atmospherics has signed a bigger contract on one condition above all: nobody but Cirrine and Apogee may read its commands, not even someone with a dish listening to the uplink. With Mirela leading and reviewing, you learn each tool by protecting something real: numbers nobody can guess, fingerprints for delivered files, signed commands nobody can extend or replay, passwords stored so every guess at a stolen copy costs 600,000 rounds, a key for every satellite from one master key, commands sealed for one reader, receipts anyone can check, a key agreed over an open link, Apogee's own certificate authority, and TLS with both ends proven. Every chapter shows the mistake first, then the way that refuses it. The season ends on Rotation Day: every key in the network replaced, at every station, without missing a single pass. Standard library only, every check on your own Mac.
