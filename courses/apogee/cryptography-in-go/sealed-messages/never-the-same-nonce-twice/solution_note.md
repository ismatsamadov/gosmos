Each call now takes 12 fresh bytes from `crypto/rand`, so two commands sealed under Cirrine-1's key are XORed with different streams, and XORing their sealed bytes leaves nothing readable. The nonce isn't secret, which is why `sealCmd` returns it to go up beside the sealed bytes: `Open` needs the same one. A repeat would cost more than secrecy, too: two GCM seals under one key and nonce also give away what an attacker needs to forge tags.
