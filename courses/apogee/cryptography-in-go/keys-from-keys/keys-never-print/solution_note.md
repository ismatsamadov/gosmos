`fmt` asks a value for `GoString` under `%#v` and for `String` under `%v`, `%+v` and `%s` before printing its fields, so under those verbs a key now prints its ID and never its bytes, alone, in a slice or in an exported field. A value inside an unexported field is out of `fmt`'s reach, which is why the ring describes itself with both methods rather than letting `fmt` walk into its versions.
