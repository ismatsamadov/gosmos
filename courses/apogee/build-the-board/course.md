The Board, the big screen in the Fishbowl that lists the next passes, is a laptop plugged into a television, and the night shift wants it on every screen in the building. Over one week at Port Ellery you build it as one program, a lesson at a time: a page made from a template that shows whatever customers write as text, each station's wind and who is on call, the same data as JSON that answers "nothing new" when nothing is, a stream that pushes every change, one source fanned out to forty screens without waiting for a slow one, weather fetched once a minute, and streams that say goodbye when the Board restarts. On Thursday night the whole night is checked against snapshots, and the Board is on every screen. Standard library only.
