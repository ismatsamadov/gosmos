# one-page

## Briefing

Monday 7 February, at Port Ellery. The Board is a laptop on the Fishbowl's television, and last week's reboot left the night shift without it for twenty minutes. Ingrid wants it served as a page any screen in the building can load, with customers' notes shown exactly as they were typed, each station's wind and who is on call.

## Debrief

By evening the Board is a page: the next twelve passes in order, Hollis's note as plain text, five wind panels and Nkem on call. Ingrid watches it reload on the kitchen screen every fifteen seconds. "Copy. Tomorrow it stops blinking."

# always-up-to-date

## Briefing

Tuesday 8 February. The Board now runs on the kitchen screen, and it blinks every fifteen seconds as it loads itself again, whole. When Cape Wren's dish stows during a pass, fifteen seconds is too late anyway. Ingrid wants each screen to fetch only what changed, and to hear about a change the moment it happens.

## Debrief

The kitchen screen has stopped blinking, and it changes within a second of each refresh. Ingrid hangs a second screen in the meeting room for the night shift. "Copy. Tomorrow, forty."

# forty-screens

## Briefing

Wednesday 9 February. Overnight the meeting room and the kitchen showed different Boards, each missing changes the other had. Ingrid wants every change on all forty screens by evening, and no screen able to hold the others up: the reception laptop goes to sleep whenever nobody touches it.

## Debrief

Forty screens show the same Board, and a sleeping laptop drops off and catches up when it wakes. On Thursday at 08:00 the cleaners switch them all on, and not one shows the 07:59 Board. Nkem: "Works. And it says what it means."

# night-shift-ready

## Briefing

Thursday 10 February. Tomás's old weather service now answers twenty calls a minute from the Board alone, and tonight's deploy at 05:00 must not wait on forty open screens. Ingrid wants the Board started exactly as the night shift will start it, and the reception screen fit for a visitor: Hollis comes in at 09:00 on Friday.

## Debrief

All night every screen showed the same Board: Cape Wren's dish stowed at 04:40, the 05:00 deploy said goodbye and came back, and Hollis read reception's screen at 09:00 with no notes in sight. Ingrid unplugs the old laptop. In March: Ystmark's new dish controllers.
