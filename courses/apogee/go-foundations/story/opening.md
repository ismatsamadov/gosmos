# Five Dishes, One Planet

kind: text
eyebrow: WHAT WE DO
action: Meet the team

A ground station is a big radio dish that talks to satellites, and we have five. A satellite stays in a dish's view for about ten minutes at a time. That window is a **pass**: our only chance to talk to it. Miss one, and the next is ninety minutes away. So our motto is *every pass counts*.

## Ystmark

label: YST
subtitle: An Arctic island

Most of our satellites fly over it on almost every lap. Foxes chew cables there, so the cables run through fox-proof pipes.

## Port Ellery

label: PEL
subtitle: Headquarters, on the roof

The only dish you can walk to. Every new program is tried here first.

## Salar Quieto

label: SQT
subtitle: A salt flat 3,840 meters up

The lowest horizon and the thinnest air we have, and two dishes called La Grande and Chiquita.

## Lantern Atoll

label: LAN
subtitle: A tropical island on the equator

Palm trees on the horizon, power from a diesel generator, and a caretaker who lives there.

## Cape Wren

label: CWR
subtitle: The far south, where nobody lives

The windiest place we own. Its dish parks itself when the wind reaches 72 km/h.

# Five Words You'll Hear Today

kind: text
eyebrow: OPS TALK, IN PLAIN WORDS
action: Get my first assignment

The Fishbowl talks fast, and these five words cover most of it. Every other word is in the Field Guide, and a lesson links to it the first time it comes up.

## Acquisition of signal

label: AOS
subtitle: The start of a pass

The moment a satellite rises into a dish's view and the two can start talking.

## Loss of signal

label: LOS
subtitle: The end of a pass

The moment it sinks below the horizon and goes quiet until next time.

## Telemetry

label: TLM
subtitle: The satellite's health report

The numbers it sends down so we know it's well: its battery, its temperature, what it's doing.

## Uplink

label: UP
subtitle: From the ground to space

Sending a satellite its instructions. Every command we send is signed, so nobody else can send one.

## Downlink

label: DOWN
subtitle: From space to the ground

Bringing down what a satellite collected: photos, files and measurements for our customers.
