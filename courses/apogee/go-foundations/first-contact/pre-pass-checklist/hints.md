Which line should the output start with, and where in `main` is the call that prints it right now?

Go runs the calls in `main` from top to bottom, so moving a whole `fmt.Println` line up or down moves its line of output with it.

The title is the only line without a step number. Below it, put the calls whose text starts with `1.`, `2.`, `3.` and `4.`, in that order.
