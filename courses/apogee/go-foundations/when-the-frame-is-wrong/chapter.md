title: When the Frame Is Wrong
position: 12
---
Errors: returning and checking `error` values, messages with `fmt.Errorf`, sentinel errors and `errors.Is`, wrapping with `%w`, custom error types with `errors.AsType`, `defer` for cleanup, and `errors.Join`. Includes programmer-error panics, recovery at a decoder boundary, and re-panicking unknown failures.
