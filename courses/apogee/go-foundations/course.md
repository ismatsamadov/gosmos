You've just joined Apogee, a small space company whose five radio dishes, from an Arctic island to a windy cape near Antarctica, talk to satellites as they fly overhead. Over one season you go from printing your first message to Pipit-1, Apogee's little test satellite, to running a whole day of satellite passes at once through a storm. Along the way you plan when each dish listens, check the health reports satellites send down, keep track of the satellites and the stations, sign the commands that go up, put downloaded files back together and handle the problems that wake the engineer on call. Every lesson is one idea and one small, real task, checked on your Mac with nothing but Go's standard library.
